3D Scene Style

What kind of 3D elements should float behind the hero? This sets the visual tone for the whole site.

A: Abstract Geometric

Slowly rotating wireframe shapes — icosahedrons, tori, rings. Clean, minimal, techy. Think luxury tech brand meets automotive.

B: Metallic Particle Field

Drifting metallic particles and dust motes with subtle amber lighting. Atmospheric, like carbon dust in a workshop. Moody and immersive.

C: Carbon Sphere & Orbs

Large distorted metallic sphere with carbon fiber texture and smaller orbiting orbs. Bold, sculptural, product-material inspired. The geometry IS the brand.
